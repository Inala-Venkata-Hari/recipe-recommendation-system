Smart Cart

Recipe recommendations and inventory-aware discounts

Offline presentation · 12 November 2026

  • Three demonstrations: original recommender → synthetic workflow → real reference-data integration.
  • Goal: connect useful meal suggestions with transparent stock-aware promotion decisions.

The problem

Two sides of the same shopping decision

Customer + seller

  • Customers have selected items and need to know what else to buy.
  • Sellers hold batches with different ages, expiry dates and excess-stock risk.
  • The project links recipe coverage to useful published offers, purchase quantities and a readable invoice.

The discount policy

Transparent rules with margin protection

An interpretable heuristic, not a trained demand predictor

  • Urgency combines age (35%), expiry (40%) and excess-stock risk (25%). Missing signals are renormalized.
  • Corpus-frequency multipliers range from 1.0 to 1.3; recipe coverage is not sales demand.
  • Maximum discount and minimum seller margin constrain quotes. Only published, valid offers reach checkout.

Correct quantities and prices

Recipe use differs from what must be purchased

A single consolidated row per product

  • Recipe weights show one serving based on declared servings or an explicit portion estimate.
  • Purchase quantities round up to full packs; the cart reports leftovers.
  • Invoices combine combo and extra quantities, identifying discounted grams.
  • Full purchased quantities are checked against usable tracked stock; catalog-only availability is unverified.

Evidence and limitations

Demonstrate the result without overstating it

All three demo workflows verified with outbound connections blocked

  • Original selection, synthetic combo checkout and real-data automatic discounts/invoices passed offline workflow checks.
  • USDA formulations may contain cooked or packaged components; historical price estimates are not live grocery quotes.
  • CPI uses a pinned September 2026 snapshot verified 8 November; seller prices remain separate.
  • No real payment, stock deduction, measured waste reduction or validated real-world demand forecasting is claimed.

Supervised ML experiment

Learn the inventory risk score

Fractional logistic regression · simulated sales outcomes

  • Predict the fraction of stock unsold over up to seven days, using stock, recent sales, age, expiry and recipe frequency.
  • 10,800 simulated examples; product-disjoint training, validation and test sets. Labels are simulated unsold outcomes, not copied rule scores.
  • Held-out test MAE: 8.6 percentage points; constant-average baseline: 29.7 points. This measures simulation performance only.
  • The ML score replaces manual priority weights. Discount caps and margin protection remain explicit business rules.
